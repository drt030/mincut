## Business gates — PRE-REGISTERED 2026-06-10, before launch (no moving goalposts after)

**Reframe:** the launch is a market TEST riding a rare attention wave, not a product launch. Fixed cost ≈ 0; the spend is owner-evenings + tokens. Revenue is the thermometer; the email list, follows, and any public forward-pick hit are the asset. Cost cap for the experiment: **2 more weeks of evenings, no paid ads** — then these gates decide.

**Hypotheses and thresholds:**
- **H-attention:** ≥1,500 unique visitors within 72h of the launch thread (all sources). Below → distribution, not product, is the problem.
- **H-demand:** ≥25 qualified paid-intent events in week 1 (founding-interest clicks, waitlist replies naming a paid domain, or unlocks if the next paid domain is live) AND paid-intent click→commit ≥5%. Below 5 qualified events → strong negative on this packaging.
- **H-asset:** email signups ≥4% of uniques; X follower delta tracked per drop.

**Decision tree (first read Sun 6/15; final read Wed 6/18 after Show HN):**
- **SCALE** (H-demand pass): weekly emerging-domain cadence locked; week-2 test = convert founding/drop interest to **$5/mo living-map subscription** (recurring matches the weekly-drop rhythm).
- **PIVOT-PACKAGING** (attention pass, demand fail): keep AI compute and parcel free, maximize list+follows; re-test monetization as subscription-on-updates for under-disclosed domains; price probe only on the first paid emerging-domain drop.
- **PIVOT-DISTRIBUTION** (attention marginal, <1,500 uniques): freeze all engineering except content; run 3 distribution experiments (hooks, zh channels, mid-size FinTwit collabs) before writing more code.
- **PARK** (after HN: <500 uniques AND <5 sales): stop the commercial push honestly; repo stays a portfolio piece, the decomposition+eval pipeline is reusable for the next vertical. Post-mortem; no zombie mode.
- **PIVOT-PLATFORM** (signal: post-launch inbound skews to "how did you build this / can I publish my own map" from analyst/builder types rather than "sell me the map"): the agent-native audience doesn't want to buy research — they want the format. Direction: open the schema, let others run decompositions through our pipeline + verification + UI and publish; charge for hosting/verification/pro. Network effect lives in the shared-subtree graph (chains interlink) — 10 domains by us is content, 100 by a community is a moat. Week-2+ north star, zero work this week beyond watching for the signal.
- **Monetization stack (strategy-migrated 2026-06-12):** AI compute becomes the free trust asset; paid discovery moves to under-disclosed emerging domains. **Commission = a free option, not a bet** — keep the inbound line + Tally form (30 min cost) but expectations LOW: custom-research buyers pay for brand/accountability we don't have yet, and the X-native audience is exactly the DIY-with-agents crowd. Owner outreach downgraded to optional. Week-2 options by signal: founding stream for humanoid/world-model/fusion maps, $39 "report edition" packaging test, 知识星球 ¥199/yr if zh traction shows (industry-research framing, no tickers, 不荐股), $5/mo living-map subscription if H-demand passes.
- **Value-layer doctrine + paywall evolution ladder (strategy-migrated 2026-06-12):** four layers — decomposition structure (easily DIY-able → free forever, the viral surface) · supplier exposure (now free in the over-disclosed AI-compute flagship; selectively paid only in under-disclosed future domains) · verification (hard to DIY → ALWAYS FREE TO SEE: visible evidence/review-states/eval scorecard are what make paid streams credible) · freshness (impossible to DIY one-shot → the endgame paid core). **Copy reframe before launch:** AI compute sells trust by being complete and free; future paid offers sell a verified living map + updates for domains the market has not already mapped to death. Free weekly email digest starts week 1 (it is the future subscription's demo). Non-gating UI task: per-domain "Updated <date> + what changed" line (freshness made visible; Sunday if it threatens the gate). **Ladder: v1 = AI compute full-free trust demo + waitlist/founding signal → v2 = first under-disclosed emerging-domain paid drop → v3 (platform signal) = generation opens up, the verification pipeline + publishing is the product.**
- - **Open-source/donations (considered + rejected as launch model, 2026-06-11):** free+tips replaces the WTP sensor with a goodwill sensor (~0.1–1% tip conversion ≈ $100 at baseline traffic, zero pricing information) and is the one-way door (free→paid burns the community; paid→free is celebrated and fully informed). Open source is filed where it works: (a) PIVOT-PLATFORM = open schema/pipeline, sell hosting+verification+freshness; (b) **Show HN option for Tue: open-source the viewer, keep verified data feeds paid** — strong HN narrative, gives away no business.
- **Risk reversal for the next paid drop (locked 2026-06-11, strategy-migrated 2026-06-12):** keep simple impulse pricing/founding pricing for the first paid emerging-domain test; at zero brand trust the binding friction is trust+card-entry, not a small price delta. The lock CTA + checkout carry a 7-day no-questions refund line ("reply to your receipt — full refund, keep access"). Cheaper-price probe remains exactly where it belongs: the PIVOT-PACKAGING branch, decided on funnel data, not pre-launch nerves. Price-sensitive segment is served by referral unlocks once a paid domain exists.
**H-demand alternative pass:** ≥2 qualified commission inquiries in week 1 counts as a demand pass even if retail unlocks miss — institutional interest is a (better) money signal.

**Bear case on record (argue with it, not around it):** the meme's originator gives research away free to 700k followers; retail pays for conviction, not tools; free tracker sites exist (semiconstocks.com, aibottlenecks.app); our distribution is zero; **and agent adoption commoditizes one-shot research — anyone on X can have an agent sketch a plausible chain in an hour.** Counter-bets: when generation is free, VERIFICATION becomes the product (review ladder, 576 whitelist evidence records, gate reports, held-out validation, forward picks) — plausible-but-unverified maps flooding the timeline make the checked one MORE valuable, not less; freshness (a living weekly-updated map vs a chat-session snapshot); AI compute as full-free trust proof; follow-up waitlist/research beats for less mapped domains; the Layer-3 forward-pick lottery ticket. **Launch messaging leads with this positioning: "Your agent can sketch this chain in an hour. It can't give you cited evidence on every node, honest review states, and a held-out validation score. Generation is cheap now. Verification isn't."**
