# Thermal Cooling Decomposition - Research Synthesis

## Level-1 Module: thermal_cooling
Parents: ai_accelerator_module_hbm_cowos

### Sub-layers to investigate:

#### 1. Module-Level Thermal Interface Components
- Thermal interface materials (TIM) - die to spreader
- Heat spreaders / thermal spreaders (copper or aluminum)
- Thermal lids / caps (direct-contact cooling interface)
- Substrate-side thermal vias and structures

Key suppliers historically:
- Shin-Etsu Chemical (TIM market leader, ~40% global market share)
- Henkel/Bergquist (adhesive TIM, ~20% share)
- Indigo Orbix (specialized TIM)
- Momentive
- Huitian (China TIM)

#### 2. Liquid Cooling Loop Components (Module-to-Rack)
- Pump/circulation unit (sealed, high reliability)
- Cold plate / water block (module interface)
- Tubing (flexible silicone or hard plastic)
- Manifold (distribution on module or rack)
- Radiator / heat exchanger (rack-level)
- Filter and particle trap
- Connection fittings

Key suppliers:
- Asetek (sealed loop leader, ~60% market datacenter liquid cooling)
- CoolIT Systems
- Lytron/Boyd (heat exchangers)
- Aavid Thermacore
- 3M (earlier NOVEC fluids, now licensing)

#### 3. Rack-Level Thermal Infrastructure
- Cooling distribution unit (CDU)
- Rear-door radiator or in-row coolers
- Chiller for large-scale infrastructure

Key suppliers:
- Vertiv (infrastructure)
- Schneider Electric (thermal management)
- Dell/HPE (integrated solutions)
- Liquid cooling fluid providers (Dow, BASF)

### Critical Research Directions:
1. What were the shipping/allocation constraints 2021-2025?
2. Current capacity (units/year) for:
   - Module-level thermal interface materials
   - Sealed liquid cooling blocks
   - Premium radiators and heat exchangers
3. Lead times for scaling (6-18 months typical for thermal component expansion)
4. Geopolitical/concentration risks
5. Double-demand scenario: what breaks first?
